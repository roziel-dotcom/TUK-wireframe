FAQ's

The straight answers

01 Who is TUKO for? +
Dads first, that's where the engineering starts. But it's built for the whole family to share the carry, however your family is built.
02 £220: is it actually worth it? +
One carrier, 4 to 36 months, instead of buying two or three as your baby grows. Founding-run pricing is below the £220 RRP.
03 When does it ship? +
First carriers ship November 2026. The founding run opens before that, with first access and founding-run pricing locked in.
04 How do I know it's safe? +
TUKO complies with CEN/TR 16512:2015, the European baby-carrier standard, and ships with fit videos built around the T.I.C.K.S. babywearing checks.
05 What's it made of? +
51% nylon, 48% polyester, 1% polypropylene, with natural-rubber parts. 92% recycled materials, working toward 100%.
